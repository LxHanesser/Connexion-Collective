# Advertising and Affiliate Disclosure

**Connexion Collective — Advertising and Affiliate Disclosure**

- Effective: 25 October 2026
- Last updated: 3 November 2026

## 1. Current status

As of the effective date of this notice, the Site:

- **carries no advertising**
- **contains no affiliate links**
- loads no ad network, ad exchange, or cross-site tracking script
- does not sell or share visitor data with third parties for advertising

If you see a commercial promotion claiming to represent the Site, please verify it with us using section 6.

## 2. If advertising or affiliate links are introduced

We may introduce advertising or affiliate links in the future to offset running costs. If we do, the following rules will apply.

### 2.1 Clear labeling

- advertisements will be labeled "Ad" or "Sponsored"
- affiliate links will be labeled "affiliate link", with a clear notice on the page
- affiliate links will carry `rel="sponsored nofollow"`

### 2.2 Editorial independence

Advertising and affiliate relationships will not influence what we write, how we review products, or the conclusions we reach. We do not accept payment in exchange for positive coverage; where an article involves a commercial partnership, the partner and the form of the partnership will be disclosed at the top of the article.

### 2.3 EU/UK: prior consent

In the EEA and the UK, non-essential advertising cookies and device identifiers will only be set after consent. If a platform such as Google is used, we will use its **certified consent management platform (CMP)**, supporting TCF v2.2 and GPP signals.

The consent banner will offer "Accept all", "Essential only", and a way to change the choice later; refusing will be as easy as accepting.

### 2.4 California: opt out of sale or sharing

California residents may use the **Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information** link in the footer, or contact us under section 6. We also honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals.

### 2.5 EU Digital Services Act (DSA): commercial communication disclosure

Where advertising is shown in the EU, we will disclose, in line with Article 26 of the DSA:

- that the content is a commercial communication
- the identity of the natural or legal person on whose behalf it is displayed
- the purposes and parameters used to target it

## 3. Data that advertising would involve

This may include page path, coarse location (country/region), device type, browser information, advertising identifiers, and consent state. The exact categories depend on the platform used and will be added to the [Privacy Policy](/docs/info/privacy) before any advertising goes live.

## 4. Advertising we will not accept

- pop-ups that hijack navigation, forced redirects, or ads disguised as system dialogs
- ads containing malware, crypto-mining scripts, or deceptive downloads
- promotions that conflict with the Site's content or mislead readers
- formats that degrade page performance or accessibility

## 5. Changes

Before introducing advertising or affiliate links, we will update this notice and the Privacy Policy and highlight the change on the Site. If you do not agree with the new processing, you can continue browsing in "essential only" mode.
