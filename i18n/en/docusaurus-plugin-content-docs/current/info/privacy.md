# Privacy Policy

**Connexion Collective — Privacy Policy**

- Effective: 3 November 2026
- Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information Connexion Collective (the "Site") processes when you visit, on what legal basis, for how long, and what rights you can exercise — including under China's PIPL, the EU/UK GDPR, and the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the CPRA.

The Site is a static personal website with no accounts, no forms, no advertising, and no cross-site tracking. In most cases we hold no information that could identify you.

## 1. Scope and controller

This policy covers every page and browser-based tool served from `connexioncollective.com` and its subdomains.

The Site is operated by an individual ("we", "us"), who is the data controller. We are not a data broker and do not sell or share personal information in any form.

## 2. Data, purposes, and legal bases

| Context | Data | Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| Page visits | IP address, User-Agent, request path and time (recorded by our hosting provider) | Deliver content, keep the service secure and available, diagnose faults | Legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) |
| Traffic measurement | Page path, referrer, country/region, device type — **aggregated** | Understand which content is read | Legitimate interests; consent where local law requires it |
| Comments | Platform account identifier, comment text, timestamp | Display and store comments | Your submission (consent or performance of the service) |
| Contacting us | Whatever you choose to send | Reply to your question | Legitimate interests or consent |

None of this information is used for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or cross-site behavioral tracking.

## 3. Information we do not collect

- Identity details such as name, email address, phone number, or postal address (unless you choose to send them)
- Accounts or passwords (there is no sign-up or sign-in)
- Payment information (there is no paid content)
- The files you process (the tools run inside your browser; files never leave your device)
- Precise location, contacts, biometric data, or other sensitive personal information

## 4. Comments

The comment section is provided by a third-party comment service, and comments are stored in the discussions area of the public code-hosting platform that service uses.

- Commenting requires an account on that platform; your username and avatar are shown publicly with your comment
- Comments are public and visible to anyone, including visitors who are not signed in
- The platform processes your account data under its own policies; we cannot access your account settings
- To remove a comment, delete it on the platform or contact us using section 11

## 5. Cookies and local storage

- The Site **sets no cookies of its own** and writes nothing to `localStorage`
- Intermediate results in the tools exist only in memory and disappear on refresh
- Our hosting and security providers may set **strictly necessary security cookies** to keep the service available and prevent abuse; these are not used for advertising or cross-site tracking

We use no analytics, advertising, or social-media cookies. If advertising or affiliate links are introduced, this policy will be updated first and a consent mechanism enabled — see the [Advertising Disclosure](/docs/info/advertising). You can clear cookies and site data at any time without affecting how the Site works.

## 6. Recipients and international transfers

The Site relies on the following categories of service providers (processors):

| Category | Purpose | Data involved |
|---|---|---|
| Cloud hosting and content delivery | Serve pages and static assets | IP address, User-Agent, request path, timestamp |
| Object storage and media delivery | Serve images and other media | IP address, User-Agent |
| Traffic measurement | Produce aggregate visit data | Page path, referrer, country/region, device type |
| Comment service | Store and display comments | Platform account identifier, comment text, timestamp |

These providers may process data **outside your country of residence**, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or on the provider's participation in an approved cross-border framework; the specific safeguards are set out in each provider's published terms.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for their own marketing.

## 7. Retention

| Data | Retention |
|---|---|
| Aggregated traffic data | About six months, as set by the measurement provider |
| Comments | Until you or we delete them |
| Access logs | Retained by the hosting provider under its policies |
| Correspondence with us | Deleted within a reasonable period after your question is resolved |

We store no personal visitor data locally.

## 8. Your rights

### 8.1 China — Personal Information Protection Law (PIPL)

You may request information about, access to, a copy of, correction of, or deletion of your personal information; you may also withdraw consent or ask for an explanation of our processing.

### 8.2 EU/UK — GDPR

Where it applies, you have the right to:

- access (Art. 15), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17)
- restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20)
- object to processing (Art. 21), including processing based on legitimate interests
- withdraw consent (Art. 7(3)) without affecting the lawfulness of prior processing
- lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority (Art. 77)

### 8.3 California — CCPA/CPRA

Where it applies, you have the right to:

- **know** the categories and specific pieces of personal information we collect, their sources, purposes, and recipients
- **delete** and **correct** your personal information
- **opt out** of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information (including cross-context behavioural advertising)
- **limit** the use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect any)
- **non-discrimination** — exercising your rights will not degrade the service or change how you are treated

**We do not sell or share personal information** as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA.

### 8.4 How to exercise your rights

Contact us using section 11. To protect your information we may need to verify your identity first, and you may use an authorized agent. We respond within the periods required by law — one month under the GDPR, 45 days under the CCPA (extendable where permitted).

Because the Site builds no personal profiles, in most cases we hold no data that could be traced back to you. If you make a request, we will state clearly what we do and do not hold.

### 8.5 Other jurisdictions

The Site is accessible worldwide. Beyond the regions above, we handle requests in line with the following laws:

- **Brazil** — Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD): confirmation, access, correction, anonymization, portability, erasure, and withdrawal of consent
- **Canada** — PIPEDA and Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25): knowledge, consent, access, and correction
- **Australia** — Privacy Act 1988 and the Australian Privacy Principles: transparent policy, access, and correction
- **Japan** — Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI): notice of purpose, third-party provision, and cross-border transfer information
- **South Korea** — Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA): consent, access, correction, erasure, and destruction
- **India** — Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP): notice, consent, access, correction, and erasure
- **Singapore** — Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA): consent, notification, access, and correction
- **Switzerland** — Federal Act on Data Protection (nFADP): rights substantially similar to those under the GDPR

Wherever you are, you may request access, correction, or erasure using section 11, and we will not treat you differently for exercising those rights.

Local representatives: if the law of your jurisdiction requires a controller to appoint a representative, contact us via section 11 and we will arrange it.

## 9. No sale, no sharing, no targeted advertising

- We do not sell or share personal information
- We do not serve advertising based on cross-context behaviour
- We do not use automated decision-making that produces legal or similarly significant effects
- We do not discriminate against anyone for exercising privacy rights

## 10. Security

We apply technical and organisational measures proportionate to the size of the Site, including site-wide HTTPS, data minimisation, and a design that does not persist visitor data in the browser.

Because the Site stores no personal visitor data, most categories of risk simply do not arise. If a security incident were ever to affect your rights, we would notify you as required by law.
