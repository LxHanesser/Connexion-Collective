# Subscribe & updates

No push notifications, no account, no algorithm. If you want to see new posts early, RSS is all you need — your reader watches the site and tells you when something new appears.

## Feed URLs

- All posts · RSS: [https://connexioncollective.com/en/blog/rss.xml](https://connexioncollective.com/en/blog/rss.xml)
- All posts · Atom: [https://connexioncollective.com/en/blog/atom.xml](https://connexioncollective.com/en/blog/atom.xml)

Paste either address into your reader. RSS and Atom contain the same content; pick whichever your reader prefers.

## How to use it

1. Install a reader: Feedly, Inoreader, or NetNewsWire (iOS/macOS) on mobile; Fluent Reader or Thunderbird on desktop.
2. Choose "Add feed" in the reader and paste the address above.
3. After that, the reader pulls updates automatically — no need to come back and refresh.

## How often do I post?

Irregularly. I write when I can; when I cannot, it sits. This is a tree hollow, not a daily newspaper. You can leave a comment under a post, or find me on GitHub.
